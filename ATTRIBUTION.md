# Attribution

DLSS Swapper LLE is an unofficial fork of
[DLSS Swapper](https://github.com/beeradmoore/dlss-swapper), created by beeradmoore
and developed by its contributors. It is based on upstream `v1.2.5.0`
(`4c58e1969ea39b35f21754e4f81e4e3baed5f8ca`).

The multi-game selection and batch-update foundation comes from
RafaelHGOliveira's [PR #913](https://github.com/beeradmoore/dlss-swapper/pull/913).
LLE extends that contribution with its shared update workflows and interface.

Later upstream contributions include the adapted scan-loading/UI-thread fix,
Japanese and Hebrew translations, DLL catalog updates and Ray Reconstruction
Preset F. Their sources are listed in [upstream imports and adaptations](docs/COMMITS.md#upstream-imports-and-adaptations).

notices and contributor credit. Third-party licenses and acknowledgements are
included in the source tree and application packages.
